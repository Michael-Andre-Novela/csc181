## 1. User group

**Who are you designing for?**  
Visitors and Tourists.

**Why might this group need support during Diyandi?**  
Because they are unfamiliar with the city and lack local connections, these individuals need support to ensure a comfortable and well-guided stay.
---

## 2. Situation or need

**What is this group trying to do during Diyandi?**  
Tourists and visitors are consistently looking for accommodations, exploring prominent local attractions, finding engaging activities, tracking festival events, and experiencing local culture.
---

## 3. Problem or inconvenience

**What may make this task difficult, confusing, unsafe, slow, or inconvenient?**  
(Assumption) Visitors and tourists frequently struggle with finding available accommodations and verifying host authenticity, as many rely on unverified Facebook groups where scams occur. Additionally, these travelers often lack clear information on when and where local events take place. While general accommodation platforms exist, they typically focus strictly on lodging rather than local experiences and services, leaving tourists feeling lost about what to do.
---

## 4. Proposed digital solution

**What digital tool would you propose?**  

A mobile application that focuses not only on lodging, but also on local experiences and city services. The app notifies users of major festival events (pulling official schedules from the LGU) and features advertisements for local establishments.

**How would it help the intended users?**  
It helps users enjoy a fun and comfortable stay. Through the application, they can secure accommodations, receive real-time schedule updates, and discover local spots to explore, preventing them from feeling lost.
---

## 5. Things users can do

Describe **two specific actions** that users could perform using your proposed system.

1. They can book staycations and services in establishments through the app.
2. They can look up information regarding local major events.
---

## 6. Important qualities

Identify **two qualities** that would make your proposed system useful. You may consider whether it should be easy to use, fast, reliable, safe, private, accessible, multilingual, low-data, clear, or available during high demand.


### Quality 1: Convenience

**Why does this matter to users?**  

With the app, visitors can book staycations and seamlessly discover local establishments, products, services, and major events all in one place. This matters because it saves them from the hassle and confusion of searching through scattered social media channels and pages for information.

### Quality 2: Trustworthiness

**Why does this matter to users?**  

Staycation hosts, local business managers, and owners are fully verified within the app. This matters because it protects tourists from online scams and fraudulent listings commonly found on social media, giving them peace of mind and ensuring they can trust the accommodations and services they patronize.

## 7. How to tell whether the solution helped

How could you determine whether your proposed solution actually helped users?

**User Feedback and Rating:** Collect user reviews and ratings to check if visitors found it significantly easier to secure safe accommodations, track event schedules, and discover local spots without feeling lost.

**Engagement and Attendance Metrics:** Monitor whether more tourists successfully attend local festival events and patronize local establishments using the real-time directions and promotional features provided by the app.
